Candy.ai's November help says the homepage Candy Studio banner opens the media workspace for accounts with access and can send other users to subscription options. Check the title of the destination and your profile before deciding what the redirect means for you.

## Start with the single asset you intend to make

A fictional adult ceramicist needs an illustration of a table full of blue cups. Decide whether your deliverable is one still or a short rotating view. If the banner opens a checkout page, read the plan description for that action and the billing interval. Record the plan name and the intended output. Both are useful if you need to confirm access with support.

![Open the official studio entry. Identify studio or checkout. Check the intended action.](/assets/editorial-candy-studio-banner-opens-subscriptions.png)

## Resolve a mismatch through the original account

If you expected access, verify the signed-in profile against your receipt and read the current support guidance. Keep the exact destination and time in your own notes. Avoid paying twice while you determine whether access belongs to that profile.
