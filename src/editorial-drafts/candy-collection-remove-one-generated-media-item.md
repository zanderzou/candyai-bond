Candy.ai's current help describes removing an individual image or video from a character album. Open Collection, choose the character's album, and open the item you mean to remove. Use the item menu to find Delete when the title and preview match.

## Identify similar generations before choosing

If you generated several versions of a fictional adult character sketch, open the exact one and compare a visible difference such as a green coat or an extra prop. Check whether the preview is the still or the video. Preserve any own version you intend to keep before clearing the unwanted item.

![Open the correct character album. Open the exact media item. Recheck the album after removal.](/assets/editorial-candy-collection-remove-one-generated-media-item.png)

## Verify the change in the album

Return to the same album and check that the item you identified is gone. Inventory any copies you previously saved or shared separately. The Help Center describes a Collection action. Consult the privacy notice for the provider's broader processing and retention terms if your concern goes beyond which items appear in the album.
