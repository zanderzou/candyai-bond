Candy.ai's Build Your Video guide describes presets for Character, Action, Location and Partner. Start with one scene that can be represented by the choices you see. A single visible action is easier to review than a sequence that needs several characters and camera changes.

## Fit a shot to the available choices

For a fictional grown-up florist, the goal is a short clip of her carrying a bouquet across a workshop. Pick an available setting that supports the action. If adding a second character makes the object hard to see, revise the scene to one person at the workbench. Use the actual preset names in your note so you can reproduce the selection.

![Select the adult fictional role. Choose one visible action. Give the action a location.](/assets/editorial-candy-build-your-video-preset-shot.png)

## Check the result against one requirement

Before confirming generation, check the available options and cost. After you get a result, watch for the bouquet staying in the shot and the character being visible at the end. The provider says choices and generated results can vary by preset.
