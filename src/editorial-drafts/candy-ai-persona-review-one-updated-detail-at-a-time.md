Choose one fictional detail that will affect a response. Change “restores paintings” to “designs gardens,” for example. Then check which persona the interface shows for the conversation. Candy.ai's help documents persona configuration under profile settings and notes that a response or image can still be inconsistent.

The small tab points to a specific row for comparison. Pick one profile field in your own before-and-after notes so you can see whether that edit mattered.

## Ask a question related to that detail

“Which materials should I bring to the courtyard?” gives a garden-design role a chance to matter. If the reply assumes canvas restoration, check whether earlier messages still mention it. Restate the new role once in the current scene before revising several fields.

## Keep the observation small

Record the fictional field, the selected persona and the high-level result. Avoid saving private life details just to make a comparison clearer. If other details vary, return to the question and note whether the particular change you wanted became visible.

Different plan or account views may show different available fields. Look at the ones offered to you. One reply provides evidence of what it used that time, and a repeated inconsistency gives you a specific issue to review.
