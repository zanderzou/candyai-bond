**Candy.ai documents a specific reason that its only saved payment method may not be removable: an active subscription must retain one.** Read the [payment-method help article](https://everai.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/61549791594265-How-can-I-remove-my-payment-method) before treating a missing removal control as a browser fault.

Your next step depends on your goal. Replacing a card, removing an extra card and stopping the subscription are different requests. Do not add a new payment method simply to see whether a button changes unless replacement is actually what you intend.

## Identify the action that is blocked

| Your goal | The state to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Remove an extra saved method | Which method is selected and whether another remains |
| Remove the only saved method | Whether the subscription is active under the documented restriction |
| Stop another renewal | Whether cancellation is confirmed, regardless of a stored card |
| Question a charge | Which transaction is involved and who can investigate it |

The table is a reasoning aid, not a claim about all current screen variants. It prevents a card-storage question from being mistaken for proof that billing has ended.

## Use the documented account area

The help center identifies the profile's Subscription area and its Payment Method section for saved-card management. When an additional method can be removed, it describes a trash control beside the selected method.

Check the intended account and selected card before acting. Read any confirmation carefully; the goal is to remove the method you chose, not accidentally change a different account setting. You do not need to expose full card information in a public screenshot to show that a control is absent.

If your subscription is active and there is only one method, the published restriction is a plausible explanation. If the screen appears inconsistent with the policy, preserve the displayed state and ask support which restriction applies.

## Cancellation needs its own confirmation

If your real objective is to stop future renewal, use the [documented cancellation route](https://everai.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/31412787067289-How-do-I-cancel-my-subscription). Removing a saved item from a list should not be treated as the cancellation acknowledgment.

After cancellation, read the subscription status and effective date. A cancellation request and an already-ended paid period may be different states. This guide does not promise that the only-card control becomes available at a particular hour; ask support if it remains unavailable when you expect the restriction to have ended.

## Make the support request specific

State whether you are trying to remove an additional method or the only method. Include the account's displayed subscription status, the relevant effective date and the exact error or absent control. A redacted image can show the issue without including the full payment details.

Do not supply invented billing information, share a password or ask a third party to sign in for you. Keep the account and billing records needed to resolve the question. A useful outcome is a clear explanation of the restriction or completion of the intended removal, with cancellation tracked separately when that is also your goal.
