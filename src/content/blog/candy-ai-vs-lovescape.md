## The decision in one sentence

**Candy AI is the more guided companion experience; Lovescape is the more visibly connected character-to-media studio.** That distinction matters more than counting feature labels, because both products cover custom adult characters, conversation, images, voice, and video in some form.

This comparison is for adults evaluating fictional AI companions. It does not test or endorse impersonation, non-consensual media, or uploads of people who have not given permission.

## Candy AI vs Lovescape at a glance

| Decision | Candy AI | Lovescape |
|---|---|---|
| Fastest start | Strong: choose from a broad catalog | Strong: browse community characters |
| Customization | Guided appearance, personality, relationship, interests, voice | Detailed appearance, personality, relationship, voice, response and sample-chat controls |
| Conversation | Core product with text, voice and call features | Core product with contextual chat and voice |
| Visual workflow | Images, video and Live Action around the companion | Character-led still generation and evolving video tools |
| Cost model | Premium plus tokens for selected actions | Premium plus Chips, especially for generation |
| Best for | Polished companionship with a low-friction entry | Directing continuity across a creative media loop |

Feature availability and prices change. Verify the official product and checkout before paying.

## 1. Character discovery and creation

Candy AI gives two obvious entry routes. You can select an existing realistic or anime companion, or build a custom fictional adult by choosing appearance, personality, relationship, interests, and voice. That makes it useful when you do not yet know whether you prefer browsing or detailed setup.

Lovescape also offers discovery and creation, but its controls read more like a reusable production brief. Official guidance describes realistic or anime styles plus appearance, personality, relationship, voice, response length, and sample chat. That extra direction can matter when identity continuity across media is your main goal.

**Practical test:** create the same fictional adult on both platforms with two appearance traits, two personality traits, one boundary, and one setting. Do not rely on the default profile text.

## 2. Chat, memory, and voice

Candy AI puts companionship at the center. Text chat, voice messages, and beta calling create several levels of presence. A voice feature is only valuable, however, if the character remains coherent and the metered cost feels reasonable.

Lovescape’s strength is the handoff from conversation to creative direction. If chat establishes a specific outfit, location, or mood, test whether the generated media preserves it.

For either platform, run ten messages and score:

1. Does it remember a preference after five turns?
2. Does it respect a clearly stated boundary?
3. Does it initiate without becoming repetitive?
4. Does voice preserve the written personality?
5. Can you remove the conversation and account without contacting support?

## 3. Images and video

Candy AI promotes image and video generation as part of the companion relationship. That is convenient for users who want media without switching to a separate creator product. Live Action and related media modes add another route from a character into visual output.

Lovescape more explicitly frames the character, still, and motion tools as one production sequence. It may be the better fit when your goal is to iterate on a visual identity rather than occasionally request a picture during chat.

Neither marketing page can prove consistency. Generate one full-body daylight scene, change only the clothing color, then create one short clip from the better still. Compare face, hands, clothing, background, motion, and the exact paid currency consumed.

## 4. Pricing: subscriptions are only the first number

Candy AI’s official subscription page lists several recurring Premium terms and describes included monthly tokens. Selected voice and media actions can spend tokens, so a low monthly-equivalent headline does not reveal the full cost.

Lovescape similarly combines Premium access with Chips. Its official guidance says Chips are used mainly for image generation and may be purchased by active subscribers.

Build a four-week estimate before choosing:

- subscription amount charged today;
- renewal date and full renewal amount;
- included currency;
- two voice sessions per week;
- two images and one video per week;
- likely retries for failed or inconsistent generations.

## 5. Privacy and consent

Both services can process highly personal conversation and generated media. Use invented details, a unique password, two-factor authentication when available, and only fictional adults. Never upload a real person unless you created the media or have explicit permission and the platform allows the intended use.

Provider claims such as discreet billing or encrypted transactions are not the same as independent verification. Read the current privacy notice for data categories, retention, processors, international transfers, deletion rights, and the identity of the billing descriptor.

## Verdict

Choose **Candy AI** for a polished, companion-first path with a large catalog, guided customization, voice, calls, and media in one relationship interface.

Choose **Lovescape** for a more creator-directed path where one reusable fictional character moves from profile to chat to still image and video.

The best answer is the product that wins your controlled test on memory, continuity, boundaries, deletion, and total cost—not the one with the longer feature list.
