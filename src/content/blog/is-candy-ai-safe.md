## Safety is a set of controls, not a yes-or-no label

Candy AI is an adult AI companion product that may process account information, conversation input, generated output, images, video, voice, usage records, and payment-related data. That does not automatically make the service unsafe, but it means the consequences of oversharing can be more personal than on an ordinary entertainment site.

The safest approach is to reduce what the account can reveal, cap what it can spend, and confirm how to remove content before creating it.

## What data should you assume is sensitive?

Treat all of the following as sensitive even if the interface feels private:

- real name, birthday, email, phone number, workplace, and address;
- conversation prompts and companion replies;
- generated images and videos;
- uploaded or recorded voice;
- character descriptions that reproduce a real person;
- subscription history and token purchases;
- device, IP, cookie, and activity records.

Candy AI’s privacy notice describes processing of registration information and content such as conversation inputs, outputs, images, videos, and voice. Read the live notice for current legal bases, service providers, transfers, retention, and rights in your region.

## Six steps before the first private chat

### 1. Use an invented persona

Do not give a companion your legal name, exact location, employer, routine, financial information, recovery answers, or credentials. A fictional nickname and broad interests are enough to test personalization.

### 2. Protect the account independently

Create a unique password with a password manager. Enable two-factor authentication if the current account settings offer it. Secure the email account used for password recovery as well.

### 3. Use only fictional adults and authorized media

Do not reproduce a real person’s identity for intimate content. Do not upload a partner, creator, celebrity, classmate, coworker, or stranger without explicit authorization. Public availability is not consent.

### 4. Check deletion before creating a library

Locate controls for conversation deletion, generated-media deletion, character deletion, and full account deletion. The privacy notice describes retention that can outlast immediate use and may include legal exceptions. If you cannot understand the deletion path, keep the test minimal.

### 5. Cap subscription and token exposure

Record the amount charged today, the renewal term and amount, the date, included tokens, and top-up price. Turn off renewal immediately if you only intend to test. Save the cancellation confirmation.

### 6. Keep local devices private

Lock your phone and computer, hide notification previews, avoid shared-browser password saving, and sign out on devices other people use. Discreet billing does not hide browser history, email receipts, downloads, or notifications.

## Is Candy AI billing discreet?

The official product promotes discreet billing, and the subscription page displayed the descriptor “EverAI” when reviewed. A descriptor can change and may still be recognizable to someone who knows the provider. Verify the exact text at checkout and assume email receipts and account history remain visible.

Longer subscription terms may advertise a lower monthly equivalent while billing the entire term up front. They may renew for the same duration. Read the confirmation line, not only the headline discount.

## Are calls, images, and videos private?

They are not public by default simply because they occur inside an account, but “private interface” should not be interpreted as “only you can ever access or process this data.” Providers use infrastructure, employees, and service processors to operate, moderate, secure, and improve services under their policies and applicable law.

Use media mode only when wanted. The official chat interface describes a control that can stop automatic media sends and token use. Check current behavior because feature names and defaults can change.

## What about provider security claims?

Candy AI promotes encrypted transactions, GDPR compliance, and account protections. Those are relevant provider statements, but this guide has not independently audited the implementation. Good personal security still matters: data minimization, unique credentials, two-factor authentication, authorized media, deletion tests, and a budget limit.

## Red flags that should stop the session

Pause if the product or anyone using it asks for:

- passwords, verification codes, or recovery phrases;
- payments outside official checkout;
- real-person intimate uploads without consent;
- proof that a subject is under 18 or youth-coded sexual content;
- instructions to bypass moderation or identity checks;
- urgent financial help or off-platform contact;
- unclear charges you cannot reconcile with your token history.

## Bottom line

Candy AI is safer when used as an adults-only fictional entertainment service with minimal personal data and explicit spending controls. Before paying, verify the current privacy notice, checkout total, renewal, token rates, cancellation, and deletion path. If you need absolute confidentiality, do not put the information into an online AI companion service at all.
